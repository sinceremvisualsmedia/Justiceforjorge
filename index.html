A fight for justice. A legacy of truth.

In December 1997, a fatal altercation outside a Rhode Island nightclub led to Jorge M. DePina's arrest and later conviction. Disputed claim His family maintains the case involved corruption and a miscarriage of justice — allegations that have not been established by any court and remain the family's contested position, not a finding of fact.

Our mission

Preserving the record.

This project is a family-led effort to recover a case record that nearly disappeared: a vanished advocacy website, court decisions, and unfinished documentary footage. The aim is a responsible, source-labeled archive — not a verdict. Every claim on this site is marked as court record, contemporary reporting, personal testimony, or a disputed claim.

The documentary

A story they tried to bury.

THIS IS LIKE A CRIME: The Lost Archive of Failed Justice is an archival prologue, targeted at 8–12 minutes, documenting the family's effort to recover a vanished advocacy website, court records, and unfinished footage. It is presented as a surviving record, not a verdict, and ends with an open call for witnesses and material.
