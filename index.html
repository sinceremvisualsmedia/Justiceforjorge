A fight for justice. A legacy of truth.

In December 1997, a fatal altercation outside a Rhode Island nightclub led to Jorge M. DePina's arrest and later conviction. Disputed claim His family maintains the case involved corruption and a miscarriage of justice.

Jorge was paroled and released in 2020. He now lives in Cape Verde.

About the documentary

1997 follows Jorge M. DePina’s story through his perspective, family accounts, court records, recovered archive material, and new footage. Production is underway, with a pre-trailer forthcoming.

Jorge was arrested in December 1997. His trial was in 1998. He was paroled and released in 2020 and now lives in Cape Verde.
